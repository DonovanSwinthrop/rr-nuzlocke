Save states

Auto states rotate through 3 slots, and one is also saved whenever you leave the app. They are separate from your in-game save: if the app ever freezes, load the newest auto state.

Time of day

Only the game's clock changes (morning 6:00, day 12:00, dusk 18:00, night 22:00). Your phone's time stays the same. The game picks it up within a few seconds outdoors.

Theme

Save in the game (Start → Save) before exporting: the export is the game's own save file. Safari can clear a site's storage if you don't open it for a few weeks; adding this page to your Home Screen keeps it, and an exported backup is always safe.

Controller: A/Cross = A, B/Circle = B, LB/RB = L/R, Start, Back/Share = Select, d-pad or stick to move, hold RT/R2 to fast forward, LT/L2 switches between controls and overlay.

Keyboard: arrows, Z = A, X = B, A/S = L/R, Enter = Start, Backspace = Select, Space = fast forward.
